Microphone Permission

The App asks for microphone access for one purpose: to detect the pitch of the notes you play so it can show you whether your instrument is in tune.

  • Audio is processed in real time, on your device only.
  • Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent to us or to any third party.
  • Microphone access is used only while the App is open and the tuner is running.

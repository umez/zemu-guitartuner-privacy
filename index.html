Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Overview

Zemu Guitar Tuner ("the app") is a guitar tuning utility. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Audio and microphone

The app requests microphone access only while you are tuning. Audio is processed locally on your device to detect pitch. No audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted. The microphone is released when you stop or leave the app.

Data collection

The app does not collect personal data, does not require an account, and has no network sync. Settings (reference pitch, tolerance, theme, custom tunings) are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected in this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via the app store listing.
